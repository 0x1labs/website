# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is people discovering Zero One Labs and the products it creates. Visitors should quickly understand what the company is, explore current and upcoming products, and continue to each product's independent website or store destination to learn more or try it.

The site also serves people interested in the company itself, including potential future contributors visiting the Careers experience.

## Product Purpose

Zero One Labs is an independent technology company founded by three friends in Kathmandu, Nepal. The company exclusively builds and publishes its own products.

Its work prioritizes thoughtful software that adds practical, creative, playful, or everyday value. Games are one part of the portfolio, not the company's defining category. The company is actively developing additional products and needs a website that can grow and be rearranged as the portfolio changes.

The website should communicate the company's identity through a clear, positive story; make the current body of work easy to understand; and send visitors to each product's independent website or store destination.

## Positioning

Zero One Labs follows curiosity into useful products rather than selling software-development services. The company builds ideas it believes should exist, publishes them under one coherent company identity, and gives each product room to retain its own purpose and personality.

## Operating Context

The company is based in Kathmandu, Nepal and is shaped by friendship, curiosity, technology, and an appreciation of nature. Three founders make up the founding team. For now, the public story should mention three founders without publishing their names or photographs.

Products are independently presented on dedicated subdomains and may also link to their relevant Google Play, Apple App Store, or Mac App Store destinations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public-facing brand name is **Zero One Labs**.
- The legal company name is **Zero One Labs Pvt. Ltd.**, subject to confirming the exact registered spelling before launch.
- The website remains on the `0x1labs.com` domain; `0x1 Labs` and `0x1labs.com` are alternate identifiers rather than the primary displayed brand.
- The company is exclusively focused on its own products. The site should not present client services, founder consulting, MVP delivery, or an outside-project sales funnel.
- The product system must support adding, removing, archiving, and rearranging products without redesigning the site.
- Every showcased product needs a direct, accurate link to its independent product site and, when available, its store destination.
- Product statuses and availability must be verified and easy to update.
- Comet and Kairos are live on Google Play and are in review for the Apple App Store.
- HueLens and Verdict are in review for publication on Google Play and the Apple App Store.
- Lumin is currently a macOS-only product and is in review for publication; its exact store destination must be verified before launch.
- Additional products are in the pipeline and should be accommodated without inventing public details before they are ready.
- Careers remains part of the site.
- The existing Blog and client-services positioning are not part of the desired future site.

## Brand Commitments

- The company should feel curious, thoughtful, human, optimistic, and serious about craft without sounding like a generic startup or agency.
- The story should connect Kathmandu, nature, friendship, and technology without relying on tourist imagery, mountain-as-success metaphors, or decorative cultural references.
- The experience should feel playful and memorable while remaining clear and easy to follow.
- The three-founder origin may be described, but founder names and photographs should not be published yet.
- The current mascot should not be treated as the primary face of the company.
- Each product keeps its own independent identity and destination while clearly belonging to Zero One Labs.

## Product Principles

1. Build for real value: every product should make some part of life more useful, clear, creative, focused, or enjoyable.
2. Let curiosity lead, then verify through making and use.
3. Treat games as one expression of the portfolio, not its center.
4. Give each product a distinct voice while maintaining a clear connection to Zero One Labs.
5. Keep the public portfolio truthful, current, extensible, and grounded in demonstrable product proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The storytelling experience must remain understandable and navigable with reduced motion, keyboard input, assistive technology, and on compact mobile screens. Essential content and product links must not depend on animation or a precise scrolling gesture.
